Architecture

Customer/business problem: teams may optimise headline conversion without accounting for incentive cost and contribution. This calculator makes the trade-off visible. A real experiment still needs randomisation, sample-size planning, guardrail metrics, confidence intervals, and an agreed stopping rule.
